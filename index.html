Privacy Policy

Pagecove PDF: Scan, Sign, Merge. Your files never leave your phone.

Effective date: October 15, 2026 · Developer: Caramelia Studio
  • Your PDFs, scans, signatures and stamps are processed only on your phone. We never upload them.
  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics. We do not run any servers that receive your data.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Purchases go through Google Play. These Google services may process some data, as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
Details

1. Who we are

Pagecove (“the app”) is developed and published by Caramelia Studio (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at carameliastudio.support@gmail.com.

2. Your files

When you open, scan, sign, fill, merge, split, protect, watermark, compress or convert a document, all of that work happens on your device.

  • Files you open (from your phone, Google Drive or another app) are copied into the app’s private storage on your phone, so the original is never changed. These working copies are temporary and are cleaned up automatically.
  • Each result is saved only where you choose, using Android’s own file picker, or shared only to the app you pick.
  • Passwords you type to open or protect a PDF are kept in memory only. They are never stored, logged or sent anywhere.
  • We never upload, read, scan for content, or keep copies of your documents.

3. Information stored on your device

To make the app work, some information is stored locally on your phone. It never leaves your device through our code:

  • Your recent files list (file names, page counts and links to the files you chose)
  • Saved signatures and stamps you create
  • Custom ink colors and app settings (for example Appearance and page setup)
  • Whether you have bought “Remove ads”, so the app can stay ad-free when you are offline
  • Simple counters used to space out ads (for example how many files you have saved), so ads are not shown too often

You can delete your recent files, signatures, stamps, colors, settings and temporary files at any time in Settings → Clear everything. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Files you saved to your own folders are never deleted by the app.

The app does not use Android backup: this information is not copied to your Google account backup.

4. Information we collect

We, Caramelia Studio, do not collect personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics or tracking tools of our own, and no servers of ours that receive data from the app.

If you email us (for example through Send feedback or Email us), we receive your email address and whatever you write. If you keep “Include app & device info” switched on, the email also contains the app version, Android version and phone model. You see the full email in your own email app before you send it, and nothing is sent unless you send it. We use these emails only to reply and to improve the app.

5. Google services used by the app

The app uses the following services provided by Google. When they are used, Google may collect and process information under its own policies. These are the only parts of the app that use the internet.

Google AdMob (advertising)

Shows a banner ad on some screens and, sometimes, a full-screen ad after you save a file. It is never shown before a file is saved, and never after you buy “Remove ads”. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your choices and where you live, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Google User Messaging Platform (consent)

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and some US states), the app asks for your consent or choices about ads using Google’s consent form. Your choices are stored on your device and shared with Google’s ad services so they can be respected.

Google Play Billing (“Remove ads” purchase)

The one-time “Remove ads” purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only receives the purchase status from Google Play, so it can remove ads and restore your purchase on your other devices.

Google Payments Privacy Notice

Google Play In-App Review

If you tap Rate Pagecove, Google Play may show its rating window. Your rating and review are handled by Google Play.

Google ML Kit Document Scanner

When you use Scan, the camera screen is provided by Google Play services. The pages are captured and processed on your device and returned only to the app. Google Play services may download the scanner the first time you use it.

Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

6. Your ad choices

  • Consent: if a consent form applies to you, you can change your choices any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown when it applies where you live).
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your phone’s settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on your phone).
  • No ads: the one-time “Remove ads” purchase removes all ads in the app.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security and retention

Your documents and app data stay on your device, protected by Android’s app storage. Temporary working files are deleted automatically, and outputs you don’t save are removed within a day. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to help you, and then deleted. Information processed by Google services is kept according to Google’s policies.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA or Indonesia’s Personal Data Protection Law), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not keep personal information about you, most of these requests are best handled on your device (Clear everything, or uninstalling the app) or with Google for its services. You can always contact us and we will help.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes on Google Play.
